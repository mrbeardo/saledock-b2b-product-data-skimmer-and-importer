# Changelog

All notable changes to **Saledock B2B Product Data Skimmer & Importer** are documented in this file.

## [v1.0.2] - 2026-11-15 (Build 2026.11.15.4)

### Added
* **The Cycle Division WooCommerce B2B Adapter:** Automatically detects `thecycledivision.com` and extracts SKUs, titles, net trade prices, stock counts, brands, categories, product URLs, and images from category grids while ignoring promotional carousels.
* **Smart Bulk Pack, Length & Volume Auto-Detection (`detectWholesaleUnit`):**
  * Automatically splits multi-packs and workshop boxes such as `(50/BOX)`, `144/Box`, `Box of 10`, `Pack of 25`, `10 pack`, `2pcs`, `x 20`, and `Gross` (144 units) into single retail units.
  * Automatically splits bulk cable/housing/tape lengths (`10m`, `30m`, `50m`, `100m`, `25m Reel`, `30m Coil`) into per-metre pricing and appends `[Per Metre]`, while ignoring `mm` dimensions (`40mm`, `180mm`).
  * Automatically splits bulk workshop fluids and pastes (`1L`, `2L`, `5L`, `1000ml`, `2000ml`) into `100ml` retail/workshop units and appends `[Per 100ml]`.
* **Missing-RRP Retail Pricing Calculator:**
  * Preserves official distributor RRPs whenever displayed on the page.
  * Calculates missing or zero RRPs using `(Unit Net Cost * 1.20 VAT) * (1 + Markup % / 100)`.
  * Added **Recommended Markup Tiers (33%–56%)** based on single-unit net cost (`<£3`: 56%, `£3–£9.99`: 50%, `£10–£29.99`: 42%, `£30–£74.99`: 36%, `£75+`: 33%) alongside a custom markup override option in `popup.html`.
* **Persistent Pricing & Pack Audit Log:** Upgraded IndexedDB (`DB_VERSION = 2`) with an `auditLogs` object store recording timestamps, original pack cost, detected pack quantities, matched rules, VAT-inclusive unit cost, applied markup %, and RRP source (`Official RRP` vs `Auto-Calc` / `Manual-Calc`), with one-click CSV export.

### Changed
* Updated `popup.html` and `popup.js` with live supplier adapter badges, markup mode controls, and an instant sample calculation preview box after skimming.

## [v1.0.1] - 2026-11-15 (Build 2026.11.15.2)

### Added
* IndexedDB storage (`SaledockSkimmerDB`) for persistent, deduplicated product storage keyed by SKU.
* Scientific-notation barcode repair (`cleanBarcode`) to fix spreadsheet-corrupted EAN/UPC codes (e.g., `2.1E+11` -> `210000000000`).
