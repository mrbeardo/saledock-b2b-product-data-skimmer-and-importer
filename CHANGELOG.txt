SALEDOCK B2B PRODUCT DATA SKIMMER & IMPORTER - CHANGELOG

[v1.0.1] - 2026-11-15

---------------------

ADDED:

- Automatic Export Tagging: Added a `Tags` column to the Saledock CSV export 

  (`buildSaledockCSV` in `extension/background.js`) that automatically appends 

  `B2B-Skimmer` to every exported product row for easy filtering and bulk 

  management in the Saledock back office.



CHANGED:

- Pipe-Delimited Multi-Value Formatting: Updated multi-image and multi-tag 

  fields to join using Saledock's `" | "` (space-pipe-space) delimiter standard.



[v1.0.0] - 2026-11-15

---------------------

INITIAL RELEASE:

- Manifest V3 Chrome/Edge extension architecture.

- Local IndexedDB storage engine (`SaledockSkimmerDB`) for high-capacity 

  product queuing and deduplication.

- Dual-mode B2B product extractor supporting Schema.org JSON-LD blocks and 

  generic DOM product card fallbacks.

- Automated scientific-notation barcode repair and wholesale multi-pack unit 

  cost/RRP splitting.

- Saledock `ProductImportTemplate` CSV generator and back-office OOSP/Publish 

  toggle macro.
